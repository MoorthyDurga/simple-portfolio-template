01 · Robotics & human-machine interaction

BrainCo Hand Teleoperation

A 20-DoF teleoperation system built by integrating a white-labeled sensor glove with a BrainCo hand.

I white-labeled and integrated the sensor glove with a BrainCo hand, then connected the system to our custom OS and ROS 2 stack. I am currently extending the integration to the Piper AgileX arm, pictured in the demo, and scaling the workflow to Wuji hands and Franka arms (not pictured) using the Wuji SDK and retargeting algorithms.

02 · Clinical research presentation

AUA 2026 · Optical Tracking Metrics

I presented our work on optical tracking metrics and surgical time during flexible ureteroscopy for kidney stone treatment at the American Urological Association (AUA) 2026 meeting in Washington, DC.

The study connects gaze behavior, including area of interest, gaze distance traveled, and fixation count, with operative duration. My contribution included processing and interpreting gaze data to examine how visual attention relates to surgical performance.

03 · Wearable sensing & connected systems

Driver Readiness

A completed anomaly-detection project focused on identifying drowsiness from public datasets.

I trained an ML model for anomaly detection to detect drowsiness from public datasets. I also built the supporting prototype around an ESP32-WROOM-DA module, MAXIO heart-rate / HRV sensing, and a skin-humidity sensor, with an Arduino programming interface, BLE connection, and cloud UI.
